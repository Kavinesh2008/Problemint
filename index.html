Built for Institutions

Everything You Need

Designed so students and staff get help fast, administrators stay organized, and problems don't repeat.

AI-Assisted Problem Solving

Before submitting, get instant suggestions from verified previous solutions. Many common issues can be solved immediately without waiting.

Smart Department Routing

Problems are automatically assigned to the right department and person. No more chasing down who to contact.

Transparent Complaint Tracking

See exactly when your complaint was seen by the admin, who's working on it, and every step of the journey.

Automatic Escalation

If a department doesn't respond within the deadline, the problem is automatically escalated to senior management.

Verified Resolution

You confirm whether the solution actually worked. No more closed tickets that weren't really fixed.

Prevention Intelligence

Patterns in complaints create prevention recommendations, helping the institution fix root causes before they affect everyone.

Privacy & Transparency

Your Information is
Handled With Care

Secure Authentication

Your password is hashed and never stored in plain text. Sessions expire on logout.

Full Visibility

You can see exactly who viewed your complaint and when — complete transparency at every step.

Role-Based Access

Each user sees only what they need. Students see their own complaints; admins see their department.

Real Data Only

Every statistic, timestamp, and AI response is based on actual records — no fabricated data.
